🤖 Native Android Detection
A Kotlin foreground service polls Android's UsageStatsManager — detects banned-app usage in real time, no root needed, low battery cost.
◈ Real XRPL Transactions
Every penalty fires a real Payment transaction on XRPL — verifiable on-chain. Group treasury is a real wallet address, not a database row.
🏦 Custodial Treasury
An omnibus XRPL treasury holds every stake; an internal ledger redistributes penalties instantly with no per-event gas. Payouts settle on-chain at session end.

Self Bets · Second Product Pillar

No group? Bet on yourself.

Not everyone wants to police friends. Self-Bets flips accountability inward: link an account you already trust with your habits, set a goal, and stake XRP or in-app tokens on your own progress. Hit the goal before the deadline → stake back. Miss it → stake forfeited. The chain doesn't take excuses.

  • 🎯
    Verifiable goals, not honor system — progress is read straight from each provider's API. No self-reporting, no cheating.
  • ⬡
    Real escrow on XRPL — XRP stakes are escrowed to the app treasury on-chain; won bets refund automatically, lost stakes are forfeited.
  • ◈
    Tokens or XRP — practice risk-free with in-app tokens, or put real crypto on the line.
  • ⚡
    Solo onboarding — a complete loop for a single user, no friends required. Removes the cold-start problem of group apps.

Why Blockchain

You can't dismiss an on-chain penalty.

Fiat penalty apps fail because charges are slow, reversible, and easy to charge back. XRPL makes the consequence instant and irreversible — that's the whole product. Every penalty is a real, verifiable Payment transaction.

⚡
3–5s Finality
Penalties settle before the guilt fades — far faster than card rails that take days to clear.
🪙
<$0.001 Fees
Sub-cent fees make micro-penalties (0.5 XRP) economically viable at scale.
🔒
Irreversible & Verifiable
No chargebacks, no "dismiss". Every penalty is public and auditable on-chain.
  • 💎
    XRPL Wallet — Each user gets a wallet; seed stored securely in Android Keychain.
  • ⚡
    4s Settlement — XRPL's 3–5s finality means penalties hit wallets faster than guilt sets in.
  • 📊
    Full On-Chain Record — Every penalty on XRPL + Supabase. Full audit trail per session.
XRPL Payment Flow
Group Creation
Host sends stake XRP → group treasury (XRPL Payment tx). Treasury address stored on Supabase + XRPL ledger.
Members Join
Each participant sends their stake XRP via direct Payment tx. Confirmed in <5s. No intermediary.
Violation Detected
Foreground service fires → server records a penalty that debits the violator's stake into the treasury. Real XRP, real consequence.
Session End
Treasury pays out on-chain to the cleanest participants — multisig-secured, with a reconciliation cron for custody integrity.
